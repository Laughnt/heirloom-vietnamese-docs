# Juicer

Heirloom Mixology's Juicer blends three fruit or vegetable items into two bottles. Use Heirloom Mixology with a matching Core build that includes the Juicer.

## Build and blend

1. Place a Dropper facing upward, clear Glass above it, and any carpet on top.
2. Right-click the glass with produce to insert one item at a time. Add exactly three; repeated or mixed ingredients work.
3. Right-click with an empty hand to start the ten-second blend.
4. When the liquid settles, right-click with a Glass Bottle to collect one juice. The liquid level falls to half.
5. Use a second Glass Bottle to collect the remaining juice.

No Water Bottle or bottle insertion is needed before blending. Before the first pour, left-click cancels and refunds the ingredients. After a pour, left-click discards the remaining juice without refunding consumed ingredients.

## Pure juice and mixed blends

Use three matching produce items for a pure juice used in recipes. Mixed batches blend their colours and list their ingredients. Juice inherits food effects, including harmful ones, and provides half the combined input nutrition. Drinking returns a Glass Bottle.

Choose the three ingredients independently in the in-game recipe browser. Bring your pure juices to the [Mixology Table](mixology-shaker.md), or [grow and harvest fruit](mixology-fruit-harvesting.md).
